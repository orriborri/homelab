# Homelab

Terms for the personal knowledge system hosted in the homelab.

## Language

**Second brain**:
The durable collection of personal knowledge, retained source evidence and its
organizational registry.

**Vault**:
The canonical collection of readable JDex notes and documents within the second brain.

**Evidence**:
Retained original source material that supports a factual claim, including its
identity and source version.

**Proposal**:
A generated note or update awaiting validation and reviewed publication into the vault.

**JDex registry**:
The existing mapping of exact JDex IDs to category names and canonical locations.
Category membership and publication permission are separate decisions.
